# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- Nothing is pre-selected after a scan.
- "Move to Trash" acts only on items that are selected and visible.
- Scans can be cancelled; a newer scan replaces an older one.
- The entry cap is reported when results are partial.
- Cache detection works at any depth below the chosen folder.
- Added: duplicate finder, old-files category, sorting, CSV export.
- Logic moved to the unit-tested `DustPilotCore` module; CI added.
